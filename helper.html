Link Launcher – Setup Helper

Create a launcher URL from a published Google Sheets CSV. The helper does not upload or save what you enter.

Important: this creates an unlisted access link, not a password. Anyone who gets the complete launcher link can open the public CSV. Do not publish personal, student, or otherwise confidential data this way.
